## Verified working (no issue)

**Cross-country isolation**
- A uae order, return, product, ad, flash sale or booking token is 404 on omn-ar.
- Carts, wishlists, wallets and addresses are separate per country for the same global account (per ADR 5).
- uae panel and ops tokens are refused on omn.
- A uae cart token gives an empty OMR cart on omn, and listing 1 can't be added there.
- An Oman gift card is refused in the UAE.
- The newsletter token from uae is invalid on omn.
- The admin panel on omn returns 404 for uae ids.
- uae-only panel members can't switch to omn.

**IDOR (all return 404 or 422, with no data change)**
- Customer B on A's order, payment, payment retry and verify, and address.
- Customer A on qa3's return, review item, notifications (mark read) and device revoke, and on B's classified ad (edit, photo, contract).
- Guests on bookings with no, wrong or another booking's token.
- Agent on another agent's assignment.
- Vendor A on vendor B's sub-order or listing.
- Travel agency on another agency's booking.

**Money**
- OMR and KWD amounts have 3 decimals (gift card 12.345 OMR purchased, captured and redeemed into the wallet exactly). 12.3456 is refused, and so is a 3-decimal amount in AED. COD fees are 1.000 OMR and 0.500 KWD.
- Flash-sale per-customer limit; cancelled flash units are released (sold_quantity back to 7).
- Single-seller COD fee.
- Refund on cancel of a captured card order.

**Flows**
- Browse, search and facets, with validation of sort, price range, per_page, rating and array params.
- PDP, guest cart and merge (normal case).
- Card checkout with the fake gateway, return redirect (doesn't trust the query string) and verify.
- COD checkout, customer cancel before shipping, and refusal after shipping.
- Return request: quantity limits, wrong item, IDOR.
- Reviews: only delivered items, once per item. Q&A (sign-in, minimum length).
- Wishlist (idempotent), notifications and unread count.
- Profile PATCH validation; email change refuses a taken email.
- Travel D8 guest booking, `X-Booking-Token`, resend-link email and the storefront `?token=` link page.
- Viewing and reserve request (guests allowed, past times refused).
- Post ad: photo required, dynamic attribute validation, then `pending_review`.
- Partner wizard for every type, request changes, resubmit and approve (panel agent).
- Advertise request (consent required).
- Newsletter double opt-in (idempotent confirm, same answer for known addresses).
- Delivery flow:
  - assign, accept, start, arrive, confirm with `codes_match` (no code required or accepted);
  - proof by signature or photo, and COD proof enforced;
  - fail with a reason; refused delivery or 3 failures trigger return to origin; reschedule rules.
- Ops APIs for vendor, supervisor, travel and marketer: role scoping, tenant 404s, `transition_refused`, stock and capacity validation, logout revocation.
- Panels: all GET routes for 6 panels plus 3 restricted admin roles; CSRF 419; logout; admin times in Oman time.

**Website**
- `dir`/`lang` are correct on all 333 route URLs and about 880 crawled pages.
- No raw i18n keys and no error digests.
- The 3-level crawl found no real 404/500 links. Oman empty states render.

## Could not test, and why

- **Coupons (valid codes), Nawy One purchase and benefit, Nawy Credits spent at checkout, contract gate on classified ads:** nothing can create coupons, plans or contract versions through the product (QA-011). `contract_versions` is empty and no classified category has `requires_contract`. Only refusal paths were tested. Credits exist only in Oman (12.345 OMR), where no product can be listed.
- **3-decimal cart/checkout totals (OMR/KWD/BHD/JOD):** no catalog exists outside the UAE, and none can be created (QA-011). Only gift card, payment-method, COD-fee and wallet amounts were checked.
- **True concurrency (stock oversell, double redeem):** `php artisan serve` handles requests one at a time, so parallel holds (12 × qty 3 on stock 10 → 3 accepted, 9 held) passed but don't prove row locking under real parallelism.
- **Token expiry:** tokens have no expiry (QA-036); only revocation was tested.
- **Live media and real gateways:** LiveKit and HLS playback, Reverb broadcasts and real gateways weren't tested. The payment return and webhooks were tested with the fake driver only.
- **Signed-in website flows in a browser:** account pages with data and checkout through the UI. The API behind them was tested directly; the website pages were checked as a guest and by curl.
- **Brand, classified shop and referral pages:** there's no data for them (`brands` is empty, and there are no shops or referral codes).
- **Operations, marketing and vendor_relations admin roles; admin staff management:** these roles weren't signed in. There's no staff or role screen, so restricted admins were created with tinker the way `tests/Pest.php` does.
- **Rate-limit precision:** the buckets are shared per IP between the three parallel testers (see QA-015), so login throttles were re-measured after a 61 s cooldown.
