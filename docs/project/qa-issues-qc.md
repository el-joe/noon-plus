**Biggest gaps by area**
- **Admin back office:**
  - No catalog, brands or categories screens; no coupons, gift cards or vouchers; no admin users or roles.
  - No refunds, disputes or warranty-claim handling.
  - No activity log or system health pages. Several nav items are dead links.
- **Missing features:** support tickets, product comparison, bestsellers, popular searches, seller storefront, FBN (inbound, storage, packaging), warehouses and transfers, vendor subscriptions, acquisition commissions.
- **Portal and marketer pages:** the marketer, influencer, affiliate and broker portal pages are missing.

**Tooling** (all clean):
- `vendor/bin/pint --test` passed.
- `npx tsc --noEmit` in `website/` exited 0.
- `flutter analyze` found no issues in all 7 packages: customer, delivery, vendor, marketer, supervisor, travel_agency and nawy_core.

**Verified compliant**
- Email-code customer sign-in, throttled per IP and per email, with an attempt lock.
- Admin date-times are entered through `<x-ui.datetime-input>` with a live "= … Oman time" helper (`resources/js/country-datetime.js`, `CountryClock::adminTimeLabel`).
- Live media is admin-only: the only write routes are `admin.media.*`.
- Code match: there is no input, only `codes_match: true` is sent, and the code is hidden from serialisation (`DeliveryAssignment` `#[Hidden]`).
- D8 no-payment wording is correct in the backend, web and app copy.
- No card PAN, CVV or expiry fields anywhere; gateway credentials use `encrypted:array` and are hidden.
- Webhooks are HMAC- or token-verified with `hash_equals`.
- Data placement:
  - 136 of 148 models use `UsesCountryDatabase`.
  - The other 12 are central by design: contracts, document types, identity, gateway configs, templates, static pages and calendar.
- Money: no float money columns and no float money casts.
- No raw SQL is built by string interpolation, and Blade has no `{!! !!}`.
- Every `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` goes through the allow-list sanitiser.
- The Sanctum token and guest tokens live in httpOnly cookies behind a same-origin BFF with an Origin check.
- No secrets are committed (`.env*` are ignored).
- Every admin route except the dashboard, settings hub, country switch and logout has a `can:` permission.
- No `$request->all()` mass assignment.
- Uploads are validated with `image` / `mimes`, and private evidence (documents, proofs, contracts) is stored on the `local` disk.
- EN/AR key parity is 100% on the website (1,956 keys) and in all 7 Flutter tables.
- No Noon logos or assets ship anywhere.
